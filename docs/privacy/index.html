Aureline Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 25, 2026

Aureline does not collect, track, sell, or transmit personal information. The app contains no advertising, analytics, account system, or third-party tracking SDK.

Local data

Settings, synthesizer voices, and voice libraries are stored locally on your device. Files leave the device only when you explicitly export or share them using Apple’s system interfaces.

Network access

Aureline does not require a network connection and does not send application data to Hidecade Instruments.

Contact

Questions can be submitted through Aureline Support.
